## Felucca

By Leo Kuroshita (@kurogedelic), Hügelton Instruments, in `third_party/felucca/`.

| File | What it is | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `drum_voice.c` | Felucca's DRUM voices and its model kits | GPL-3.0-only | [hugelton/Felucca](https://github.com/hugelton/Felucca) `firmware/src/drum_voice.c` |
| `dsp.c` | Shared fixed-point DSP helpers | MIT | [hugelton/Felucca-Engines](https://github.com/hugelton/Felucca-Engines) `src/dsp.c` |
| `felucca_engine.h` | The engine interface `dsp.c` expects | MIT | Felucca-Engines `src/felucca_engine.h` |
| `gen_engine_tables.py` | Generator of the sine, pitch, filter and soft-clip tables | MIT | Felucca-Engines `tools/gen_engine_tables.py` |
| `fe_tables.h` | Generated by `python3 gen_engine_tables.py fe_tables.h` (44,100 Hz) | MIT | — |

The MIT licence text is in `third_party/felucca/LICENSE-MIT-Felucca-Engines.txt`.
Felucca's GPL-3.0 text is the same as this project's `LICENSE`. Commits and
SHA-256 hashes are in [third_party/felucca/UPSTREAM.md](third_party/felucca/UPSTREAM.md).

## Hortator

Hortator is a drum machine fork of Felucca by DEADACTIVE (2026), on top of
code by Leo Kuroshita, Hügelton Instruments, in `third_party/hortator/`. Its
licensing notes are kept as `third_party/hortator/LICENSING.md`.

| Files | What they are | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `dm_kick.c`, `dm_snare.c`, `dm_metal.c`, `dm_dsp.c` | The classic 808/909-style models, and the models' fixed-point toolkit | GPL-3.0-only; their integer ports of Plaits and stmlib drum algorithms are MIT (Emilie Gillet, Mutable Instruments) |
| `dm_perc.c`, `dm_fm.c`, `dm_memb.c`, `dm_state.h` | Toms, congas, claves and rim; FM percussion; the MEMB drum head; the models' voice state | GPL-3.0-only |
| `dm_ktrx.c`, `dm_sdtrx.c`, `dm_xttrx.c`, `dm_trxn.c`, `dm_rstrx.c`, `dm_trxm.c`, `dm_trxcb.c`, `dm_cltrx.c`, `dm_b2trx.c` | The measured digital (TRX) models | GPL-3.0-only |
| `dm_bdefm.c`, `dm_efmn.c`, `dm_efmm.c` | The measured FM (EFM) models | GPL-3.0-only |
| `phys_dsp.c` | The modal core under MEMB and SKIN, a fixed-point port of DaisySP | MIT (Electrosmith and Emilie Gillet) |
| `dsp.c` | Felucca's DSP helpers as Hortator ships them | GPL-3.0-only |
| `preset_bank.h` | The 151 factory presets, generated by Hortator's `tools/gen_presets.py` | GPL-3.0-only |
| `gen_tables.py`, `felucca_tables.h` | Table generator, and its output from `python3 gen_tables.py felucca_tables.h` | GPL-3.0-only |

The MIT texts are in `third_party/hortator/LICENSES/`. Hortator's `LICENSE` is
byte for byte this project's. `src/hortator_engine.c` restates the parts of
Hortator's `core.h` and `eng_sample.c` (`pow2_q16`) that the models need, and
lists the models in Hortator's `dmodels.c` order. Commits and hashes are in
[third_party/hortator/UPSTREAM.md](third_party/hortator/UPSTREAM.md).

## Talko Monk

The reverb, ring modulator, phaser and tape effects in `src/effects.cpp` and
the panel, startup and PWM code follow Talko Monk (GPL-3.0-only).

project: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Hügelton
Instruments, Felucca, DEADACTIVE or Hortator. The kit names STD, 80, 10, 66,
55 and 77 are Felucca's; the sound names in kits `6` to `F` are Hortator's
preset names.

## Sharing the firmware

A UF2 built from this folder is firmware derived from Felucca and Hortator.
Whoever receives it must also be offered this complete source, including
`third_party/`, under GPL-3.0.
